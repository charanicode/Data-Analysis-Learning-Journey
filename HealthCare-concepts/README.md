# 🏥 Healthcare Insurance Concepts

## 📚 Topics Learned

Explored the fundamentals of healthcare insurance and the terminology used in the US healthcare system.

- **Health Insurance Premium** – Regular payment made to maintain insurance coverage.
- **Deductible** – Amount a member pays toward eligible healthcare expenses before the insurance plan starts sharing costs, according to plan rules.
- **Copayment (Copay)** – A fixed amount or specified share paid by the patient for a covered service.
- **Coinsurance** – Percentage of covered medical costs paid by the patient.
- **In-Network vs Out-of-Network** – Differences between contracted and non-contracted healthcare providers.
- **Pre-authorization** – Approval required from an insurer before certain treatments or services.
- **Claim Denial** – When an insurance claim is rejected by the insurer.
- **Electronic Health Record (EHR)** – Digital record of a patient's medical information.
- **Third-Party Administrator (TPA)** – Organization that handles healthcare insurance administration and claim-related tasks.
- **Cashless Claims** – Eligible medical expenses settled directly between the insurer and network hospital.
- **Reimbursement Claims** – Process in which a patient pays the medical bill first and requests reimbursement for eligible expenses.
- **Medicare** – US federal health insurance program primarily serving people aged 65 and older, along with certain other eligible groups.
- **Medicaid** – US health coverage program for eligible individuals and families, administered under federal and state rules.
- **Pre-existing Disease** – A medical condition that existed before the relevant insurance coverage began, as defined by the policy.
- **Waiting Period** – Specified period before certain benefits or conditions become eligible for coverage.
- **Network Hospital** – Hospital contracted with an insurer or health plan.
- **Room Rent Limit** – Maximum room charge covered under a policy, when such a limit applies.
- **Out-of-Pocket Maximum** – Limit on eligible patient cost-sharing during a plan year under applicable plan rules.
- **Explanation of Benefits (EOB)** – Document explaining how an insurer processed a claim; it is generally not a bill.

Gained a basic understanding of healthcare insurance terminology, insurance coverage, patient cost-sharing, and claim processing.
